## Overview

Phase 27 implements specialized material models for photorealistic representation of anisotropic surfaces, hair, fabric, and layered materials. Supports brushed metals, cloth, hair/fur, and composite materials.

---

## Key Features

- Anisotropic GGX BRDF for brushed metals and surfaces
- Hair/fur rendering via Marschner model variant
- Cloth material BRDF with fabric characteristics
- Layered and composite materials with blending
- Smooth material transitions and crossfading
- Clearcoat layer support for varnish/polish
