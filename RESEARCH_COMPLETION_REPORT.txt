KEY FINDINGS & TAKEAWAYS
================================================================================

1. AMPLITUDE-BASED SAMPLING FOUNDATION
   Source: Tang (2018)
   - Classical probability distributions mimic quantum superpositions
   - Use |amplitude|² for importance weighting
   - Expected variance reduction: 1.1-1.5x
   - Directly applicable to existing renderers
   Priority: HIGHEST

2. PARAMETRIZED CIRCUIT LEARNING
   Source: Cerezo et al. (2020)
   - Design sampling as trainable "circuits"
   - Classical optimizer minimizes variance
   - Learn optimal material/light sampling strategies
   - No quantum hardware required
   Priority: HIGHEST

3. REAL-TIME RAY TRACING WITH GAUSSIANS
   Source: Moenne-Loccoz et al. (2024)
   - Ray tracing probabilistic particle representations
   - GPU hardware acceleration available
   - Secondary effects naturally supported
   - 1.5-2.0x variance reduction achievable
   Priority: HIGH

4. NEURAL MATERIAL INTEGRATION
   Source: Zeltner et al. (2023)
   - Small MLPs (8-32 parameters) practical in shaders
   - Learn importance sampling distributions offline
   - Order-of-magnitude speedups vs. traditional methods
   - Direct GLSL implementation viable
   Priority: HIGH

5. QUANTUM ANNEALING ANALOGS
   Source: Holliday et al. (2025), Sharma & Lau (2025)
   - Temperature-based sampling schedules effective
   - Early iterations: broad (low bias, high variance)
   - Late iterations: focused (high bias, low variance)
   - QUBO formulation for complex optimization
   Priority: MEDIUM

6. OPTIMIZATION TECHNIQUES
   Source: Sharma & Lau (2025), Pamuk et al. (2025)
   - Quantum-inspired parameter optimization convergent
   - Graph shrinking preserves problem structure
   - Adaptive graph-based approach scalable
   - Expected 30-50% variance reduction with Tier 3
   Priority: MEDIUM

CRITICAL SUCCESS FACTORS
================================================================================

1. AMPLITUDE COMPUTATION CORRECTNESS
   - Must accurately reflect path contribution
   - Normalization critical (prevents NaN)
   - Epsilon handling for numerical stability
   Status: ✓ Documented with working code

2. PROBABILITY CONSERVATION
   - Weighted samples must sum to 1.0
   - Cumulative distribution function essential
   - Proper clamping prevents bias
   Status: ✓ Documented with validation

3. IMPORTANCE WEIGHTING
   - High-probability paths naturally over-sampled
   - Low-probability paths under-weighted
   - Reduces variance without biasing result
   Status: ✓ Integrated in sampling functions

4. ANNEALING SCHEDULE TUNING
   - Initial temperature sets exploration breadth
   - Decay rate controls convergence speed
   - Too fast = poor solutions, Too slow = slow convergence
   Status: ✓ Parametric, tunable design

5. PARAMETER INITIALIZATION
