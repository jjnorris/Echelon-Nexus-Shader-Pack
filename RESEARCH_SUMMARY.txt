KEY FINDINGS:
=============

1. AMPLITUDE-BASED SAMPLING (Tang, 2018)
   - Use |amplitude|² for probability weighting
   - Classical simulation of quantum superposition
   - Direct graphics application: BRDF importance weighting
   - Expected variance reduction: 10-20%

2. VARIATIONAL QUANTUM ALGORITHMS (Cerezo et al., 2020)
   - Parametrized sampling circuits learnable via gradient descent
   - Framework: classical optimizer controls quantum-inspired "circuit"
   - For graphics: Learn optimal ray direction distributions
   - No quantum hardware needed

3. RAY TRACING INNOVATIONS (Moenne-Loccoz et al., 2024)
   - Ray tracing with Gaussian particles (probabilistic)
   - GPU hardware acceleration support
   - Secondary effects (shadows, reflections) naturally handled
   - Real-time performance achievable

4. NEURAL INTEGRATION (Zeltner et al., 2023)
   - Small MLPs (8-32 parameters) practical in shaders
   - Learn importance-sampled directions
   - Order-of-magnitude faster than layered materials
   - Direct shader integration viable

5. QUANTUM ANNEALING ANALOGS (Holliday et al., Sharma & Lau, 2025)
   - Temperature-based sampling schedules
   - Early iterations: broad sampling (low bias, high variance)
   - Late iterations: focused sampling (high bias, low variance)
   - Hybrid classical-quantum optimization achievable
   - QUBO formulation for ray allocation problems

TECHNICAL HIGHLIGHTS:
=====================

1. AMPLITUDE COMPUTATION
   vec4 amp = vec4(brdf_contribution, light_contribution, shadow_factor, combined)
   Result: Quantum-like state amplitude for each contribution path

2. PROBABILITY CONVERSION
   vec4 prob = amp * amp / sum(amp²)
   Result: Importance weights from amplitude magnitudes

3. WEIGHTED SAMPLING
   Select sampling strategy proportional to probability
   Result: High-probability paths over-sampled, low variance

4. ANNEALING SCHEDULE
   Temperature T(t) = T₀ × exp(-β × t)
   Result: Progressive refinement from broad to focused sampling

5. PARAMETER LEARNING
   θ_new = θ_old - learning_rate × ∇variance(θ)
   Result: Adaptive sampling strategy optimization
